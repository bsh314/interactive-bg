Fading out

With fadeOutBottom: 160, the pattern fades from its own opacity to nothing over the bottom 160 px of the element. The background colour stays solid. fadeOutTop, fadeOutLeft and fadeOutRight do the same towards the other edges; try them on the page background in the panel.
